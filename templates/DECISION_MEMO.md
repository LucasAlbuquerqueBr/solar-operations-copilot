# Decision memo — complete after your analysis

## Customer and decision

Who will use the product, how often, and what action will they take?

## Evidence

State measured results, evaluation period, data coverage, baseline performance, and model limitations. Link your figures and tables.

## Top investigation

Describe the evidence, alternative explanations, uncertainty, and recommended diagnostic action. Do not claim a confirmed failure without independent evidence.

## Commercial case

Show low/base/high outcomes using explicit assumptions. Distinguish historical modeled shortfall from prospective recoverable value.

## Environmental relevance

Explain the potential energy benefit. Quantify carbon only with an identified source and method.

## Pilot proposal

What would you measure during a four-week pilot? What maintenance logs, operating constraints, or sensor checks would you need? What result would make you stop or change direction?
